# Coliving in Brussels for Couples: Which Operators Accept Two?

Coliving is built around individuals — but plenty of couples move to Brussels together and want the same perks: all-inclusive rent, flexibility, instant community, and zero admin. The good news is that couple-friendly options exist. Here's how it works and where to look.

## Can couples do coliving in Brussels?

Yes — but not everywhere, and not in every room. Most houses have a mix of room sizes, and only the larger rooms or studio-style units work for two people. So the question is never really "does this operator accept couples?" but "does *this specific room* accept two?"

Expect to pay a **surcharge of roughly €100–€250/month** for a second occupant, covering the extra utilities, cleaning, and shared-space use. That's still often cheaper than a private one-bedroom once you count bills — see our [rent prices guide](/blog/brussels-rent-prices-2026).

## Operators more likely to accept couples

- **[Live Colonies](/actors/livecolonies)** — their "Super-Coloc" model explicitly accepts couples in many units, a genuine rarity. Often the first place to look.
- **[Habyt](/actors/habyt)** — offers studios and larger rooms in some buildings that can suit two, plus flexible terms.
- **[Cohabs](/actors/cohabs)** and **[Colive](/actors/colive)** — have certain larger rooms that work for couples, usually with a surcharge. Ask specifically.
- **[Co-Homing](/actors/co-homing)** and **[Morton Place](/actors/morton-place)** — more private, ensuite-heavy setups that can suit couples wanting calm.

Browse all [operators](/actors) and message a few — availability changes constantly.

## What to ask before booking as a couple

1. **"Do you have a room that accepts two people?"** (not just the operator's general policy)
2. **The exact surcharge** for a second occupant.
3. **Bed size** — is it actually a double?
4. **Ensuite or shared bathroom?** Two people sharing a house bathroom is a bigger ask; an [ensuite room](/blog/coliving-private-bathroom-brussels) is worth it for couples.
5. **Registration** — can *both* of you register (domiciliation) at the address? Essential if you're both staying over 3 months.

## Coliving vs a private flat for couples

Here's the honest trade-off:

| | Coliving (couple room) | Private 1-bed |
|---|---|---|
| Cost | All-in, + surcharge | Rent + bills + setup |
| Admin | Minimal | You handle everything |
| Community | Built-in | Build it yourself |
| Privacy | Your room only | Whole flat |
| Flexibility | High (short leases) | Low (long leases) |

For couples **new to Brussels for under two years**, coliving can be the easier landing — you get a social network from day one instead of being an isolated pair in a new city. For couples wanting total privacy or staying long-term, a [private rental](/blog/renting-in-brussels-guide) may fit better.

## The move that works for most couples

Look at [Live Colonies](/actors/livecolonies) first (couple-friendly by design), then message 2–3 other operators about their specific couple rooms. Prioritise an **ensuite** room for comfort, confirm both of you can register, and weigh the all-in couple price against a private one-bedroom with bills. Often coliving wins for the first year — then you decide.
