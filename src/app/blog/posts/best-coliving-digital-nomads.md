# Top 7 Coliving Spaces in Brussels for Digital Nomads (2026)

**Brussels as a Digital Nomad Hub?** Yes! With excellent connectivity to London, Paris, and Amsterdam, plus a thriving startup scene, Brussels is underrated for remote workers. The key is finding a place where you can be productive *and* social.

If you've ever struggled to find good WiFi in a rental apartment or felt isolated working from home alone, this guide is for you.

## What Makes a Coliving Space "Nomad-Friendly"?

Not all coliving spaces are created equal for work. Here is our criteria:
1.  **Fast & Reliable WiFi:** Minimum 100 Mbps download/upload.
2.  **Dedicated Coworking Area:** Not just a kitchen table. Proper desks and chairs.
3.  **Community of Professionals:** People who understand that 9-5 is work time.
4.  **Short-Term Flexibility:** 1-3 month leases for true nomads.

## The Best Coliving Spaces for Remote Workers

### 1. Cohabs (Various Locations)
**Global Standard:** With their beautifully designed coworking spaces in each house, you're guaranteed a professional setup. Their app also lets you connect with other members across the city for networking.
*   **Best for:** Networking & Socializing.
*   **Price:** €700 - €950 / month all-in.

### 2. Morton Place (Châtelain)
**Premium & Quiet:** Targeting a slightly older demographic (28+), Morton Place offers luxurious, spacious homes where you can actually focus. It feels more like a boutique hotel than a student dorm.
*   **Best for:** Focus & Professionals.
*   **Price:** €1,000+ / month.

### 3. Neybor (City Center)
**Sustainable & Design:** Neybor integrates urban farming and sustainability. Their city center locations often feature large communal tables that double as coworking spots during the day.
*   **Best for:** Exploring the City & Creatives.

### 4. Ikoab (Everywhere)
**Budget-Friendly:** If you are bootstrapping your startup, Ikoab offers the most affordable options without sacrificing the essentials like internet and community.
*   **Best for:** Budget & Long stays.

## Comparison: Coworking vs. Coliving

Should you get a separate coworking membership?

| Feature | Coliving with Workspace | Separate Coworking (e.g., Silversquare) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Cost** | Included in Rent | €295 - €400 / month extra |
| **Commute** | 0 minutes (Slippers!) | 20-30 minutes |
| **Networking** | Housemates Only | Broader Professional Network |
| **Vibe** | Relaxed / Casual | Professional / Focused |

## FAQs for Nomads

### Do I need a visa to work remotely in Belgium?
EU citizens do not. Non-EU citizens generally need a visa, but Belgium has introduced a specific "Digital Nomad Visa" recently (verify current laws). Also, checking coliving contracts for proof of address is essential for registration.

### Is English widely spoken?
Yes. Brussels is multilingual (French, Dutch, English). In coliving spaces, English is the *lingua franca*.
