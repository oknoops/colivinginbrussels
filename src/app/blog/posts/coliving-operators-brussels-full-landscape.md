# The Complete Map of Brussels Coliving Operators in 2026

Brussels' coliving market has quietly become one of the deepest in Europe. Where a few years ago there were a handful of players, there's now a whole spectrum — from design-led boutique houses to global tech-driven brands to accessible flatshare-style operators. This is the full map, updated for 2026, so you can see every major option in one place.

Each operator below links to our detailed profile with prices, amenities, and vibe. Prices are all-inclusive monthly rents; ranges vary by room and location.

## The premium & design-led

### [Corners](/actors/corners)
Design-forward maisonettes in prestigious neighbourhoods like [Ixelles](/neighborhoods/ixelles) and Uccle. Mid-century furniture, curated art, grown-up atmosphere. For professionals who value privacy and style as much as community. **From €700.**

### [Morton Place](/actors/morton-place)
"Boutique coliving" for a slightly more mature crowd (late 20s–30s). Luxury, privacy, wine tastings over wild parties, in upscale Saint-Gilles and Ixelles. **From €1,000.**

### [Neybor](/actors/neybor)
For the design-conscious urbanist — sustainable ecosystems with urban farming, aquaponics, and raw-yet-warm interiors. Intellectually creative crowd, stunning city-centre locations. **From €850.**

## The big, social, amenity-rich

### [Cohabs](/actors/cohabs)
The most recognisable name in Brussels coliving. Fully renovated townhouses, packed events calendar, incredible amenities (cinema rooms, gyms, rooftops). Energetic and community-driven — the place to make instant friends. **From €800.**

### [Live Colonies](/actors/livecolonies)
Purpose-built coliving with a "Super-Coloc" concept bridging flatshare and managed service. Relaxed, hassle-free, accepts couples in many units, strong digital process. **From €650.**

### [Comoon](/actors/comoon)
Amenity-heavy houses on the EU-quarter axis (Schuman, Montgomery, Schaerbeek), with coworking, cinema room, gym, and bike parking bundled in. A strong pick for people working in or around the European institutions. **From €860.**

## The global & digital

### [Habyt](/actors/habyt)
The global coliving giant. Standardised, modern, efficient, tech-first booking, and the ability to transfer between cities in their network. Reliable and consistent — great for digital nomads and short-term assignments. **From €600.**

### [Sharies](/actors/sharies)
A fully-serviced, 100% digital model across residences in [Saint-Gilles](/neighborhoods/saint-gilles) and the centre. Water, energy, wifi, cleaning, linen — even daily household products — all included, with an on-site residence manager. Zero admin, same-week move-in. **From €800.**

## The accessible & community-focused

### [Colive](/actors/colive)
A diverse network of houses across the whole city, inclusive and budget-friendly with a "family style" atmosphere. Truly all-inclusive rent (even household insurance). An excellent entry point for interns and first-timers. **From €600.**

### [Ikoab](/actors/ikoab)
One of the pioneers of Belgian coliving ("I Know A Bird"). Student- and young-professional-friendly, huge variety of houses, among the most competitive prices. Classic "Auberge Espagnole" energy with better management. **From €500.**

### [Co-Homing](/actors/co-homing)
A dozen furnished houses across the most liveable corners of Brussels (Châtelain, Flagey, Louise, Cinquantenaire). "Privacy where it counts, social when you want it" — most rooms have private bathrooms. A favourite of consultants, trainees, and graduate students who want central stability without a long lease. **From €700.**

### [Coloc Housing](/actors/coloc-housing)
The accessible, classic end of the market — furnished shared houses with flexible stays and all-in rent. Closer to a well-managed *colocation* than a heavily-branded coliving: fewer frills, friendlier prices, easy to move into for a semester or a first job. **From €600.**

## How to choose between them

With this many options, the trick is to sort by *what matters most to you*:

*   **Tightest budget?** Start with [Ikoab](/actors/ikoab), [Colive](/actors/colive), or [Coloc Housing](/actors/coloc-housing).
*   **Best social life & events?** [Cohabs](/actors/cohabs) leads, with [Corners](/actors/corners) close behind.
*   **Design & premium feel?** [Corners](/actors/corners), [Morton Place](/actors/morton-place), [Neybor](/actors/neybor).
*   **Maximum flexibility & digital ease?** [Habyt](/actors/habyt) and [Sharies](/actors/sharies).
*   **Working in the EU quarter?** [Comoon](/actors/comoon) and [Co-Homing](/actors/co-homing) sit right in that world.
*   **Want privacy (your own bathroom)?** [Co-Homing](/actors/co-homing) and the premium operators.

## A note on the wider market

Beyond these managed coliving brands, Brussels also has a huge classic *colocation* (flatshare) market, student housing operators, and serviced-apartment providers. Coliving sits in the sweet spot between them: more community and service than a flatshare, more flexibility and soul than a serviced apartment. If you're weighing the trade-offs, read our [coliving vs flatshare guide](/blog/coliving-vs-flatshare-brussels) and our [price comparison](/coliving-brussels-prices).
