# Coliving with a Private Bathroom in Brussels: Where to Find Ensuite Rooms

For a lot of people, one thing makes or breaks shared living: the bathroom. The good news is that coliving in Brussels has matured, and **private ensuite rooms** are now common — you can have the community *and* your own bathroom. Here's where to look.

## Why an ensuite changes everything

A private bathroom is the single biggest upgrade in coliving comfort:
*   No morning queue before work or a stage.
*   Your own space to actually relax and reset.
*   The privacy that makes shared living sustainable long-term.

It's the feature that turns "I could do a few months" into "I could live here for years."

## Operators with strong ensuite options

### [Co-Homing](/actors/co-homing)
Explicitly built around "privacy where it counts" — most rooms come with a **private bathroom** and dedicated kitchen storage, across a dozen central houses (Châtelain, Flagey, Louise, Cinquantenaire). One of the best picks if an ensuite is non-negotiable.

### [Comoon](/actors/comoon)
Furnished private rooms with their **own shower**, plus a gym, coworking, and cinema room on the EU-quarter axis. Amenity-rich and private.

### [Morton Place](/actors/morton-place)
Boutique, grown-up coliving where rooms often feel like **hotel suites** — spacious and private, in upscale Saint-Gilles and Ixelles. The premium end, but the comfort matches.

### [Cohabs](/actors/cohabs) & [Corners](/actors/corners)
Both renovate townhouses with a **mix** of ensuite and shared-bathroom rooms. Ask specifically for an ensuite when enquiring — they exist but go quickly.

### [Neybor](/actors/neybor) & [Habyt](/actors/habyt)
Newer, modern properties that tend to offer more ensuite rooms than older housing stock.

## What it costs

As a rule of thumb, an ensuite room runs roughly **€50–€150/month more** than a shared-bathroom equivalent in the same house. Given what you get — no queues, real privacy — most people who can stretch to it say it's the best money they spend. See the full ranges on our [price page](/coliving-brussels-prices).

## How to make sure you actually get one

Room types vary *within* the same house, so a little diligence pays off:

1.  **Say "ensuite / private bathroom" explicitly** in your first message — don't assume.
2.  **Ask for photos or a video tour** of the *specific* room, not the house generally.
3.  **Confirm it's truly private**, not a "semi-private" bathroom shared between two rooms.
4.  **Book early** — ensuite rooms are the first to go, especially in [Ixelles](/neighborhoods/ixelles) and central houses.

## The sweet spot: private room, social house

The dream setup for most newcomers is exactly this — retreat to your own ensuite room when you want to, then step out into a lively kitchen full of housemates when you want company. That balance of **privacy plus community** is what modern coliving does best, and it's very achievable in Brussels.
