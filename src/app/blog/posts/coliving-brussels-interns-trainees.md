# Coliving in Brussels for Interns & Trainees (EU Stage Survival Guide)

Every year thousands of interns and trainees land in Brussels — Schuman *stagiaires*, Blue Book trainees, NGO interns, and company placements. Almost all face the same problem: they need a nice place for **3 to 6 months**, can't sign a Belgian nine-year lease, and don't know anyone yet. Coliving was practically invented for this. Here's how to do it right.

## Why coliving beats a solo flat for a stage

*   **Flexible leases.** Traditional Belgian rentals lean long (3/6/9 years). Coliving offers 3–6 month terms that match your stage exactly. Operators like [Habyt](/actors/habyt) even do month-to-month.
*   **No guarantor drama.** Most operators skip the Belgian-guarantor requirement — a deposit and first month usually does it. See our [FAQ](/faq) on deposits.
*   **All-in, no setup.** One payment covers rent, utilities, wifi, and cleaning. You won't waste your short stay setting up an electricity contract you'll cancel in four months.
*   **Instant social life.** This is the big one. You arrive knowing no one; by week two you have housemates, dinners, and a friend group. For a short stint, that community is everything.
*   **Domiciliation.** Staying over 3 months means you'll likely need to register — most coliving addresses allow it. Confirm before signing.

## What a stage-friendly budget looks like

EU trainee grants and intern stipends are modest, so budget matters:

*   **Tightest budget (€500–€700):** [Ikoab](/actors/ikoab), [Colive](/actors/colive), and [Coloc Housing](/actors/coloc-housing) are the value champions — classic house shares, competitive all-in rents.
*   **Mid-range (€700–€900):** [Cohabs](/actors/cohabs), [Co-Homing](/actors/co-homing), and [Sharies](/actors/sharies) offer more amenities and services.
*   **If your stipend stretches (€860+):** [Comoon](/actors/comoon) puts you right on the EU-quarter axis with a gym and coworking built in.

Compare them all on our [price page](/coliving-brussels-prices).

## Best neighbourhoods for interns

*   **[Etterbeek](/neighborhoods/etterbeek)** — the strategic pick if you're at the Commission/Parliament. Walk or one tram to Schuman.
*   **[Ixelles](/neighborhoods/ixelles)** — social, central, packed with other young internationals. Place Luxembourg ("Plux") is the Thursday-night stagiaire ritual.
*   **[Saint-Gilles](/neighborhoods/saint-gilles)** — cheaper, artsy, still well-connected.
*   **[Schaerbeek](/neighborhoods/schaerbeek)** — best value, up-and-coming, good tram links.

## Practical tips for a short stay

1.  **Book early.** Stage seasons (Feb/Sep intakes) mean rooms go fast. Start looking 6–8 weeks out.
2.  **Ask about the exact end date.** Confirm your lease can end when your stage does, with no penalty.
3.  **Check what "all-in" includes.** Most cover utilities + wifi + cleaning; confirm.
4.  **Prioritise the social fit.** For 3–6 months, a house that organises dinners and events will make or break your experience. [Cohabs](/actors/cohabs) and [Corners](/actors/corners) are known for it.
5.  **Meet the vibe first.** Ask for a virtual tour and a sense of who lives there — age, nationalities, energy.
