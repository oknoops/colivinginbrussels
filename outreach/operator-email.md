# Operator outreach — backlink & listing email

Goal: get the 12 coliving operators to (1) confirm/correct their profile listing and
(2) link to their profile page from their own site (a backlink — the biggest off-page
SEO lever). Soft mention of paid featured placement.

Send from a person (hello@colivinginbrussels.com), personalise line 1, lead with the
"check your info" ask, and do ONE polite follow-up after ~5–7 days.

---

## Subject line options
1. `{{operator}} on ColivingInBrussels — please check your listing`
2. `We feature {{operator}} — quick question about your profile`
3. `Your {{operator}} profile is live on ColivingInBrussels`

## Sending tips
- Personalise line 1 with one real detail (their newest house, an event, their design).
- Lead with the correction ask, not the link — people reply to "check your info" far more.
- Send from a person, not info@.
- One follow-up after ~5–7 days: "Just floating this back up — happy to correct anything
  on your {{operator}} profile."
- Start with the smaller/local operators (Ikoab, Comoon, Sharies, Co-Homing, Coloc
  Housing, Morton Place) — they reply faster than the big chains (Cohabs, Habyt).
