## Tips
- Personalise line 1 with one real detail (an article of theirs, their tone, a recent post).
- One ask per email. Keep it under ~120 words — busy editors skim.
- For guest posts, offer to write it FOR FREE and send a draft — removes their effort.
- One polite follow-up after ~5–7 days.
- Start with the warm/easy ones (Living in Brussels, Everything Coliving, Brussels' Kitchen,
  the directories), then the bigger media.
