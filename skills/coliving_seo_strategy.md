# Coliving in Brussels: AIO/SEO Strategy

Based on the `keyword_strategy.md` framework, here is the tailored strategy for "Coliving in Brussels".

## 1. Core Pillars (Primary Keywords)
These are the high-volume terms that define the business. Target these on the Homepage and Main Service Pages.

| English | French (Fr) | Dutch (Nl) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Living in Brussels** | Vivre à Bruxelles | Wonen in Brussel |
| **Brussels Neighborhood Guide** | Quartiers Bruxelles | Wijken Brussel |
| **Moving to Brussels** | Déménager à Bruxelles | Verhuizen naar Brussel |
| **Coliving Brussels** | Coliving Bruxelles | Coliving Brussel |

**Action:** Ensure the H1 of the homepage includes "Living in Brussels" and "Guide".

## 2. Content Attributes (Secondary Keywords)
Target these on specific Guide pages or Blog posts.

- **Lifestyle:** Best restaurants, nightlife, parks, gym memberships.
- **Practical:** Registration (domiciliation), public transport (STIB/MIVB), taxes, grocery shopping.
- **Audience:** Expats, Newcomers, Students (ULB/VUB), Interns.

**Action:** Create dedicated guide pages for each of these topics.

## 3. Hyper-Local Strategy (Neighborhoods)
Brussels is a city of neighborhoods. Users search by location.

- **Ixelles (Elsene):** Target "Châtelain", "Flagey", "Cimetière d'Ixelles".
- **Saint-Gilles (Sint-Gillis):** Target "Parvis", "Louise".
- **Etterbeek:** Target "EU Quarter", "Schuman", "Merode".
- **Brussels City:** Target "Dansaert", "Sainte-Catherine".

**Action:** Create a "Neighborhoods" or "Locations" section on the homepage and eventually dedicated types of pages (e.g., `/coliving/ixelles`).

## 5. Technical SEO & Structure
- **URL Structure:** `/coliving/[neighborhood]` or `/actors/[actor-name]`.
- **Schema Markup:** Use `ApartmentComplex` or `RealEstateListing` schema for actor pages.
- **Speed:** Ensure Images are Next.js optimized (already doing this).
- **Mobile:** 100% responsive design.
