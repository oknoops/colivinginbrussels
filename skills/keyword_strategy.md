Guide: Developing a Multi-Language Keyword Strategy
This document outlines the systematic process for generating a comprehensive keyword strategy for professional services, specifically tailored for multi-lingual markets such as French, Dutch, and English.

Define the Core Pillars (Primary Keywords)
Start by identifying the fundamental services offered. These are high-volume terms that define the business and should be targeted on the Homepage and Main Service Pages.
+1

Action: List the top 5-10 services in the primary language.
+1

Translation: Translate these terms accurately into all target languages, such as "location bureau" to "kantoor huren" and "office rental".
+1

Localization: Add the primary city or district, such as Brussels or Uccle, to these terms to capture local search intent.
+1

Identify Service Attributes (Secondary Keywords)
Expand the primary list by adding specific features, amenities, or conditions that users search for. These should be targeted on service-specific pages.
+1

Amenities: Include features like "meeting rooms," "parking," or "receptionist".
+1

Conditions: Include terms like "furnished," "short-term," or "24/7 access".

Targeting: These keywords should be mapped to specific sub-pages rather than the homepage.
+1

Map the Customer Journey (Intent-Based)
Categorize keywords based on where the user is in their decision-making process.
+1

Awareness: Focus on "What is..." or "Advantages of..." to capture users researching the concept.
+1

Consideration: Use "Price," "Comparison," or "Rates" for users evaluating options.
+1

Decision: Target high-intent terms like "Cheap," "Best value," or "Reviews" for users ready to buy.
+2

Analyze Long-Tail and Local SEO
Target lower-competition phrases that answer specific questions or address niche needs, ideal for blog content and FAQs.
+1

Problem-Solving: Use "How to..." phrases, such as "how to register a company address".
+1

Hyper-Local: Include postal codes like 1180, specific street names, and "near me" variations.
+2

Audience-Specific: Tailor terms for specific personas like "freelancers," "startups," or "non-profits (ASBL)".
+2

Trust and Compliance Keywords
For regulated industries, include terms that prove legitimacy and build credibility.
+1

Certifications: Include official government bodies, such as "SPF Economie" or "FOD Economie".
+1

Legal terms: Use "Legal business address" or "Registered office" to attract professional clients.
+1

Competitor and Market Trends
Monitor the landscape to capture traffic from competitors or seasonal shifts.
+1

Comparisons: Target "Alternative to [Competitor]" or "[Company] vs [Competitor]" to capture traffic from users looking for options.
+1

Seasonality: Include time-sensitive terms like "Promotion," "New Year deals," or "September" to capture seasonal spikes.
+2

Implementation Roadmap (Phasing)
A strategy is only useful if executed in order of impact.
+1

Phase 1 (Immediate): Optimize the homepage and main service pages with primary keywords and set up Google My Business.
+1

Phase 2 (Short-term): Build local landing pages and an FAQ section targeting long-tail keywords.
+1

Phase 3 (Medium-term): Launch a blog with regular articles and start gathering customer testimonials.
+1

Phase 4 (Long-term): Create comprehensive resource guides and interactive tools like cost calculators.
