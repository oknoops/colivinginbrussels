SOP: Blog Content Generation Framework
1. Title & Meta Foundation
Every article must start with high-impact metadata that aligns with 2026 SEO standards.

SEO Title: Max 60 characters. Include the primary keyword at the very beginning (front-loading). Use "Power Words" or a year to indicate freshness (e.g., "Guide for 2026").

Clean URL: Short, descriptive slugs using only the primary keyword and essential modifiers. Remove "stop words" like 'and' or 'the'.

Meta Description: 150-160 characters. This must be benefit-driven, clearly stating what the reader will learn to encourage clicks.

2. The "AEO" Lead (Answer Engine Optimization)
Search engines in 2026 prioritize "zero-click" answers and AI snippets.

Direct Answer: Within the first 50–100 words, provide a concise 2–3 sentence answer to the primary question of the article.

Hook: Use empathetic language that validates the reader's problem (e.g., "If you've ever felt [Problem] after [Event]...").

3. Structural Hierarchy (H-Tags)
Content must be scannable for both humans and AI crawlers.

H1: The main title.

H2s for Main Concepts: Break the article into 3–5 logical sections.

H3s for Specific Details: Use these for lists, steps, or individual tips.

Formatting: Every H2/H3 section should be followed by short paragraphs (max 3 sentences), bullet points, or numbered lists to avoid "walls of text".

4. The "Value Stack" Content Requirements
To build topical authority, the article must include specific high-value elements:

Science-Backed or Expert Insights: Cite studies, biological processes, or industry experts to satisfy "E-E-A-T" (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness).

Comparison Tables: Whenever comparing products, ingredients, or methods, use a Markdown table for clarity.

Internal Linking: Include at least 2–3 links to other relevant "Spoke" or "Hub" articles on the site using descriptive anchor text.

Actionable "How-To" Steps: Provide a clear, numbered list of actions the reader can take immediately.

5. Visual & Interactive Elements
Image Alt-Text: Every image must have descriptive alt-text including a relevant secondary keyword.

FAQ Section: Conclude with 3–5 frequently asked questions using Schema-ready headers. This helps capture "People Also Ask" snippets in search results.

6. Tone & Governance
Voice: Natural, conversational, and authoritative. Avoid "AI-isms" like "In the rapidly evolving landscape...".

Closing (CTA): End with a clear Next Step. Whether it's a product recommendation or a newsletter sign-up, the article must drive the user further into the funnel.
